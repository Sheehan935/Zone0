AB 3074 Frequently Asked Questions

Understanding California's Zone 0 mandate for defensible space, ember resistance, and compliance timelines.

What is Assembly Bill 3074 (AB 3074)?
AB 3074 requires the Board of Forestry and Fire Protection to establish regulations for an ember-resistant zone (Zone 0) within 0 to 5 feet of all structures located in Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zones (VHFHSZ).
Are bark and wood mulch allowed in Zone 0?
No. Organic mulches such as wood chips, bark, and pine needles ignite easily when struck by windborne embers. They must be replaced with non-combustible materials like gravel, decomposed granite (DG), pavers, or bare earth.
Do I have to remove all plants from the 5-foot perimeter?
Not necessarily. While minimizing plants directly adjacent to walls is recommended, low-growing, high-moisture specimens planted selectively with non-combustible groundcover separation are allowed depending on local fire authority enforcement.
Will non-compliance affect my home insurance?
Yes. Major insurance providers operating in California increasingly require proof of Zone 0 compliance and defensible space maintenance prior to policy renewals or issuing new coverage in high-risk zones.
