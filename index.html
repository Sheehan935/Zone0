System Overview

Defensible Space & Native Plant Integration

Four zones, radiating out from the structure — each with different rules for what can grow there.

House 100+ ft
Zone 0 0–5 ft from structure

Ember-resistant zone. Non-combustible gravel, pavers, and hardscape only — no woody plants touching the structure.

Zone 1 5–30 ft

Lean, clean and green. Low, well-spaced, well-irrigated native plants with no ladder fuels.

Zone 2 30–100 ft

Reduced fuel zone. Spaced shrubs, limbed-up trees, and regularly mowed grasses.

Zone 3 100+ ft

Wildland transition. Existing vegetation thinned and cleared of dead material, not newly planted.

Self-Audit Guide

5-Step Zone 0 Inspection Checklist

Follow this sequential process to audit your property perimeter before an official Cal Fire or insurance compliance inspection.

01 Ground

Clear Organic Mulch

Remove all bark, wood chips, pine needles, and organic matter within 5 feet of walls.

Non-combustible ground only
02 Hardscape

Install Gravel / DG

Replace organic mulch with crushed stone, river rock, gravel, or decomposed granite.

Minimum 2" depth rock
03 Flora

Prune & Thin Plants

Remove woody brush directly beneath windows and prune lower tree limbs under 6 feet.

Eliminate fuel ladders
04 Roof & Gutters

Clean Ember Traps

Clear dead leaves from gutters, roof valleys, and under balcony deck joists.

1/8" metal mesh guards
05 Storage

Relocate Combustibles

Move firewood piles, plastic furniture, and wooden planters out of the 0-5 ft perimeter.

Move 30+ feet away
