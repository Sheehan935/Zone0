All plants will eventually burn. There is no such thing as a fireproof plant. Defensible space is about managing fuel, moisture, and plant placement.

Wildfire Ember Dynamics

Why the 5-Foot Zone Matters Most

Embers travel up to 2 miles ahead of a wildfire front. Over 90% of structure ignitions occur when blowing embers land in organic bark mulch or dry vegetation directly touching house siding.

90%

Perimeter Ignitions

Homes rarely ignite from radiant heat alone. They burn when wind-driven embers accumulate in organic bark chips, wood piles, or woody shrubs directly touching foundation walls.

0%

Organic Bark Chips

Cal Fire AB 3074 prohibits combustible wood mulch within 5 feet of structures. Replacing bark with decomposed granite or gravel creates an active ember break.

100%

Curb Appeal Preserved

Defensible space does not mean concrete. Flagstone walkways, crushed stone, and fire-adapted California succulents maintain a sophisticated aesthetic.
