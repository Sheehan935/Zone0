The Science of Ember Dynamics & Ignition Vulnerability

During a wildfire, it is rarely a giant wall of flame that burns down a house. It is the invisible blizzard of burning embers blowing miles ahead of the fire front.

How Embers Behave

Embers are driven by high winds and act similarly to blowing snow. Wherever snow drifts and piles up around your house—like corners, stairwells, and against the foundation—is exactly where burning embers will accumulate.
