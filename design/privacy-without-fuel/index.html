Privacy Without Fuel

How to design beautiful, noncombustible privacy screening within the 0–5 foot defensible space perimeter.

1. Metal Panels

Laser-cut steel or aluminum screens offer full sightline blockade with zero fire risk.

2. Breeze Blocks

Architectural concrete masonry creates wind-tolerant, ember-proof patio enclosures.

3. The 5-Foot Break

Replace wooden fence attachment points with metal gates to sever the fuel line to your home.
