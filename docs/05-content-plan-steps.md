# Phase 3: Build the Lead Magnet

Goal: use educational content to increase qualified Tally submissions.

Asset:

- "5-Step Zone 0 Inspection Checklist"

Format options:

- PDF checklist
- email follow-up after Tally submission
- printable webpage
- downloadable guide after email capture

Content sections:

1. Clear organic mulch.
2. Install gravel, DG, pavers, or stone.
3. Prune and thin plants.
4. Clean ember traps.
5. Relocate combustibles.
6. Take proof photos.
7. Verify local requirements.

Conversion path:

1. Homepage explains the checklist.
2. Visitor submits the Tally photo-check form.
3. Confirmation page tells them what happens next.
4. Follow-up email sends checklist and next-step offer.

- What is the follow-up email sequence?
