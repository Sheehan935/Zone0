# Instagram Business Account Setup

## Step 1: Account Creation & Setup
1. **Download the App:** Get Instagram on your phone (management is mobile-first).
2. **Create the Base Account:** Tap "Sign Up" and use your business email.
3. **Trigger the Switch:** Go to your profile, tap "Edit Profile", and select "Switch to professional account".
4. **Choose Category:** Search for and select "Landscape Company" or "Home Improvement".
5. **Select "Business":** Choose Business (not Creator) to unlock local ad tools and analytics.
6. **Add Contact Details:** Enter your business email, phone number, and location.
