Understanding Defensible Space Zones

Protecting Bay Area homes through NFPA guidelines and California fire codes.

Zone 0: Ember-Resistant Zone (0–5 Feet)

The most critical zone directly surrounding your home. Goal: Prevent embers from igniting direct contact materials.

  • Use hardscaping, pavers, or gravel instead of organic mulch.
  • Remove combustible plants, firewood stacks, and debris near walls.
  • Ensure clear space beneath decks and eaves.

Zone 1: Lean, Clean, and Green (5–30 Feet)

Designed to stop fire spread and reduce radiant heat intensity near structures.

  • Maintain well-irrigated, low-growing fire-resistant vegetation.
  • Space trees to prevent continuous canopy fire paths.
  • Remove dead leaves, needles, and dry vegetation regularly.

Zone 2: Reduced Fuel Zone (30–100+ Feet)

Focuses on interrupting wildfire progress and creating a defensive perimeter.

  • Clear vertical ladder fuels (trim lower tree branches up to 6 feet).
  • Keep wild grasses mowed down to 4 inches maximum height.
