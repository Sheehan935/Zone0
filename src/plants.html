Bay Area Fire-Resistant Plant Guide

Recommended plants for Defensible Space Zones 1 & 2 (5–30+ ft from structure) selected for high moisture content and low ignition rates.

Succulents & Groundcovers

Examples: Agave, Sedum, Delosperma (Ice Plant)

High moisture retention; ideal for planting near outer hardscape boundaries.

Native Perennials

Examples: California Poppy, Yarrow, Salvia (Sage)

Drought-tolerant, low sap/resin content, and adaptable to Bay Area soils.

Fire-Safe Shrubs

Examples: Ceanothus (California Lilac), Manzanita (low-growing varieties)

Requires minimal pruning and maintains leaf hydration throughout dry summers.
